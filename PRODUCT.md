# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Angular 19 Standalone Components, CSS Custom Properties, LocalStorage with JSON backup & restore, Vercel deployment.

## Users
Professionals, students, and productivity enthusiasts seeking an ultra-responsive, bilingual (Thai & English), distraction-free daily planner and habit orchestrator.

## Product Purpose
Todaprime helps users eliminate daily cognitive overload and conquer procrastination by streamlining daily task execution, habit routines, and time management into a singular high-craft, intuitive interface.

## Positioning
Unlike generic task managers or cluttered kanban boards, Todaprime combines daily planning with intelligent habit tracking, day-range task management, multi-device sync, and zero-latency instant offline-first operation.

## Operating Context
Daily desktop and mobile browser workflows. Used across varied lighting conditions (dark/light mode switchable), with frequent rapid task input, status triage, and reflection.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Daily agenda planning & timeline
- Day-range scheduling (today to future target date)
- Habit tracking with streak monitoring
- Instant deferral / re-scheduling (e.g., ⏩ Tomorrow)
- 100% completion celebration & motivation
- Bilingual i18n support (English & Thai)
- Local storage persistence & JSON backup/restore
- Responsive mobile & desktop support

## Brand Commitments
Name: Todaprime
Tone: Crisp, focused, empowering, refined, uncluttered.

## Product Principles
1. Instant Focus: Zero cognitive friction from thought to task capture.
2. Calm Clarity: Avoid visual noise; elevate readable typography and clear visual hierarchy over superficial decoration.
3. Tactile Feedback: Every action acknowledges user intent with crisp micro-interactions.
4. Inclusive & Global: Native bilingual support (Thai & English) with flawless Thai typographic legibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Full WCAG AA contrast compliance (>= 4.5:1 text contrast), responsive tap targets (min 44px on mobile), clear focus-visible outlines, and bilingual labels for all screen readers.
